Program 01 · Warrant comes first

Development

In Scotland a building warrant has to be in place before work starts, not approved as you go, and a completion certificate has to be submitted and accepted before anyone occupies the finished building. That is a different procedure from the English one and it changes the programme, because the consent sits at the front. We do not start warrantable work without a warrant. A date that depends on a consent nobody has yet is not a date, it is a hope.

Program 02 · Missives bind

Buying and selling

Two Scottish differences to know. A Home Report, covering a survey, an energy report and a property questionnaire, has to be available before most homes are marketed, so you see the survey before you offer rather than after. And missives bind both sides once concluded, which is earlier than people moving from England expect, and is the reason gazumping is rare here. Get a Scottish solicitor involved before an offer goes in, not after.

  • As principal
  • Home Report provided
  • Solicitor essential
  • No agency fees

Program 03 · No end date

Letting

A private residential tenancy here has no fixed term and no expiry date. There is no no-fault notice in Scotland, so recovering a property needs a statutory ground and a tribunal order, which is a genuinely different bargain from an English tenancy and worth understanding from both sides. Deposits go into an approved Scottish scheme within the statutory period, counted in working days. Property is kept to the repairing standard.

  • As landlord
  • Open-ended tenancy
  • Deposit protected
  • Repairing standard

Program 04 · New, and saying so

What we are not

This company was formed in July 2024 and we do not claim a history we do not have. We sell and let as principal, so we are not an estate agent or a letting agent, we hold no client money and we claim no agent redress scheme. We do not forecast value, rent, yield or resale, because nobody can and those who do are selling something. Where a structural warranty exists on a property we will name the provider, and where none exists we will say so.

  • Formed 2024
  • Principal, not agent
  • No forecasts
  • Cover named or denied
